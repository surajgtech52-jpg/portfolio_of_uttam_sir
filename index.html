Research Focus

📡
Wireless & Mobile Communications

Mobile Ad Hoc Networks, Wireless Sensor Networks, 5G mobility management, femtocell resource allocation, and handover decision systems.

🧠
AI & Machine Learning

Deep learning applications, neural network-based control systems, and intelligent algorithms for network optimization and mode-switching.

🔒
Secure Routing & Opt.

Trust-based secure routing in MANETs, hybrid optimization algorithms, call admission control, and topology-hiding multipath protocols.

🧬
Biomedical Signal Processing

EEG artifact suppression using wavelet ICA, ocular and EMG artifact filtering, and multichannel signal acquisition techniques.

📊
Cognitive Radio Systems

OFDM-based resource allocation, hybrid optimization models, dynamic group search techniques, and spectrum management.

🎓
Engineering Education

Project-based learning in Indian technical education, CBCS syllabus design, academic governance, and pedagogy innovation.
